*** Snowsql and PUT command ***


- Command to create the BIKE_STAGE:

	CREATE OR REPLACE STAGE DEMO.DEMO_SCHEMA.BIKE_STAGE;
	

- More examples on the PUT command ( Mac/Linux or Windows): https://docs.snowflake.com/en/sql-reference/sql/put#examples


- Command used to PUT the bike dataset files from my local folder into the stage BIKE_STAGE - To adapt to your local folder containing the datasets:

	PUT 'file:///C:/Users/d.weigel/Downloads/2023-citibike-tripdata/*/*.csv' @BIKE_STAGE
